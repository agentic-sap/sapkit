FUBRs aren't maintained in a SPRO customizing view; they're application-side planning settings. You maintain them via **Planning → Planning Settings → Freight Unit Building Rule** (NWBC, or the Fiori "Define Freight Unit Building Rules" app in S/4HANA TM). The related customizing, such as freight unit types, is under *SPRO → Transportation Management → Planning → Freight Unit*. I'm answering from memory, so check the exact menu path in your release.
