Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung is filed electronically (ELSTER) **monthly, quarterly, or annually**, depending on the prior year's payroll tax amount:

- **Monthly** if it exceeded €5,000 (due by the 10th of the following month)
- **Quarterly** if it was €1,080–€5,000 (due the 10th after quarter-end)
- **Annually** if it was €1,080 or less (due by January 10 of the following year)

(Newly founded businesses report monthly for the first two years in some cases.)
