Remnant management in mill-products (steel) processing deals with handling the leftover pieces (remnants) from cutting coils, plates, or bars, such as offcuts and partial coils/sheets. It covers tracking, storing, and reusing them as stock for later orders, or scrapping them, so material yield improves and waste is minimized.
