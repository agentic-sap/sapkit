Most businesses file consumption tax returns **once a year** (individuals by March 31 of the following year; corporations within 2 months of fiscal year-end). Larger taxpayers must also file **interim returns**: once a year-end tax of over ¥48 million triggers 3 interim filings (quarterly), over ¥4.8 million triggers 1 (mid-year), and over ¥480 million triggers 11 (monthly). Small businesses can opt for a special election (消費税の期限特例) to file twice a year instead.
