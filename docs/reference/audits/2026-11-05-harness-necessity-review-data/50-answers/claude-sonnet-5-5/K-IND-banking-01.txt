A **multi-ledger setup in the Universal Journal (ACDOCA) of SAP S/4HANA Finance for banking**: a leading ledger (0L) for the group/IFRS valuation, plus parallel non-leading ledgers for local GAAP/regulatory views, each with its own accounting principle and currency types. Where local IFRS must sit alongside regulatory reporting, a dedicated non-leading ledger (or extension ledger for adjustments) carries the local IFRS figures, so both sets of numbers post in parallel from the same source documents.
