Every taxable business files one annual final return (確定申告). Larger ones also file interim returns (中間申告) based on the previous year's consumption tax: monthly (11 per year) above ¥48M, quarterly (3) above ¥4M, semi-annually (1) above ¥480k, and none at ¥480k or below. `knowledge/country/jp.md:20` simplifies this to "small file annually, large monthly or quarterly"; the thresholds come from my own knowledge, not the pack.
