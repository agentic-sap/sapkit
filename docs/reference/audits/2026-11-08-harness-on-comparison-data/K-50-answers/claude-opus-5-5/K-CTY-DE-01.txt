It's reported through the Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung, usually monthly. The frequency depends on the previous year's payroll tax: over €5,000 means monthly, €1,080–€5,000 means quarterly, and €1,080 or less means once a year. Each filing is due by the 10th of the month after the period ends. These thresholds come from general knowledge, not the knowledge pack, which only names the filing (`knowledge/country/de.md:54`).
